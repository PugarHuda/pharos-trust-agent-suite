The differentiator

Everyone built a shield. We built the firewall.

The most crowded category in this hackathon is agent “safety”. Almost all of those projects return advice — a score, an ALLOW/WARN/BLOCK verdict the agent is free to ignore. A jailbroken agent ignores advice. Our boundary lives inside the contract: the treasury reverts an out-of-policy spend before it can broadcast. That is the difference between a linter and a firewall — and it is only one of eight skills.

UNIQUE #1

Enforcement, not advice

Spending limits, allow-lists and a kill-switch are enforced by AgentTreasury on-chain. A compromised prompt physically cannot exceed the policy — proven by a live blocked spend tx.

UNIQUE #2

Un-fakeable reputation

Reputation is payment-gated: only the address that actually paid (EIP-712 signed, keyed by (payer,ref)) can rate. A relayer can’t forge it; a non-payer’s rating reverts NotPayer. Anti-sybil by construction.

UNIQUE #3

Breadth × depth × proof

Others are broad-but-shallow or deep-but-narrow. We are the only entry high on all three axes at once: 13 skills, 236 tests, 11 live contracts, and a complete on-chain commerce loop.

UNIQUE #4

Composition wired in code

treasury shells to shield pre-flight; strategy emits treasury.executeCall calldata; stylus risk-gates a spend; mesh routes x402 settlement through the policy. Not a diagram — actual call paths.

UNIQUE #5

Complete ERC-8004 + x402

The only entry implementing the full ERC-8004 trio — Identity + Reputation + Validation — plus x402 gasless payments. The reputation score is readable via the standard getSummary() on-chain.

UNIQUE #6

Reputation that's economic

A ReputationGate (the ERC-8183 hook live on Base from Virtuals + the Ethereum Foundation) forwards funds only to a counterparty whose reputation + validation clear a bar. Reputation becomes a permission that moves money — proven on-chain.
